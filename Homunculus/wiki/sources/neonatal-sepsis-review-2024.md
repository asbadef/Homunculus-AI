# Neonatal Sepsis: A Comprehensive Review (Antibiotics 2024)

**What it is.** A narrative review of neonatal sepsis — definitions (or their absence),
epidemiology, presentation, biomarkers, and emerging monitoring approaches (Kariniotaki,
Thomou, Gkentzi, et al., *Antibiotics* 2024;14(1):6).

**Authority: moderate.** A narrative review, not a consensus guideline or systematic review.
Claims taken from it should be attributed as review-level evidence. Where it reports a primary
finding (e.g. the HeRO score, presepsin performance), the primary source should eventually be
captured into `raw/` in its own right.

## Key claims it supports

- **"No clear consensus definition for sepsis in neonates exists."** → [[nicu-sepsis-gap]],
  [[neonatal-sepsis]]
- **EOS vs LOS**: EOS within the first 7 days (first **72 hours** for infants hospitalised from
  birth); LOS beyond 3–7 days. GBS and *E. coli* cause two-thirds of EOS; CoNS dominates LOS in
  high-income settings; ***Candida* is the third most common LOS cause in VLBW infants.**
  → [[neonatal-sepsis]]
- **Neonatal sepsis does not present like adult sepsis.** Temperature *instability* rather than
  fever — hypothermia especially in preterms. Apnoea, bradycardia, feeding intolerance,
  lethargy. **Hypotension is a late sign.** → [[neonatal-sepsis]], [[nicu-sepsis-gap]]
- **"Abnormal heart rates with decreased variability and transient decelerations are found to
  precede the diagnosis of LOS by hours, even days."** → [[heart-rate-variability-neonatal-sepsis]]
- **HeRO score** (2005): continuous HRV monitoring giving "early indications of sepsis prior to
  any symptoms" in VLBW, preterm and term infants.
  → [[heart-rate-variability-neonatal-sepsis]]
- **Prematurity is the single most significant risk factor** for both EOS and LOS.
  → [[neonatal-sepsis]]
- **Biomarker kinetics and limits**: CRP rises 8–12 h (useless early; two normal values give
  NPV 99.7%); PCT rises 2–4 h; presepsin rises at 2 h, peaks at 3 h (88.9% sens / 85.7% spec
  for EOS). **Neither CRP nor PCT is adequate as a single marker.**
  → [[neonatal-sepsis-biomarkers]]
- Blood culture needs **≥1 mL** — a real constraint in a neonate; culture-negative sepsis is
  common and a negative culture "does not rule out sepsis." → [[neonatal-sepsis]]
- **ML/AI for neonatal sepsis prediction is an active research area but "not yet in clinical
  application."** → [[ml-for-sepsis-prediction]]

## Why this source matters most to Homunculus

The HRV finding is the closest thing in the vault to **direct evidence for the product's core
thesis**: that a continuously-monitored physiological signal degrades measurably *hours to days*
before the clinical diagnosis is made. In the NICU it is not a hypothesis — it has a named,
deployed instrument behind it (HeRO). See [[heart-rate-variability-neonatal-sepsis]] and
[[why-early-detection-is-possible]].
