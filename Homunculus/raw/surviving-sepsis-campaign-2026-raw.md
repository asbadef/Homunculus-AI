# Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2026 — Adult Guidelines

Published March 2026 (Crit Care Med, April 2026 issue). Update of the 2021 edition, covering
evidence published through **June 2025**. Developed with GRADE methodology.

- **129 statements**, of which **46 are new** (not addressed in 2021).
- Panel of **69 people from 23 countries**; 38% currently or previously practising in a
  low- or middle-income country.

## Key recommendations captured

### Screening and early recognition

- **Use NEWS, NEWS2, MEWS, or SIRS *over* qSOFA** as a hospital sepsis screening tool —
  **strong recommendation, moderate certainty**.
- In the **prehospital** setting, employ a standardised sepsis screening tool —
  **conditional recommendation, very low certainty**.

### Initial resuscitation

- For sepsis-induced hypoperfusion or septic shock: **at least 30 mL/kg IV crystalloid within
  the first 3 hours** — **conditional recommendation, low certainty**. Clinicians should
  reassess frequently to avoid both over- and under-resuscitation.
- **Serial lactate measurements to guide resuscitation** — **conditional, low certainty**.
  Fluid administration after the initial bolus should be individualised rather than driven by
  complete normalisation of lactate.

### Haemodynamics

- **Initial MAP target 65 mmHg** over higher targets — **strong, moderate certainty**.
- **Age ≥65 years: initial MAP range 60–65 mmHg** over higher targets — **conditional, low
  certainty**. *(New in 2026.)*
- **Norepinephrine as the first-line vasopressor**, over dopamine, epinephrine, or
  selepressin — **strong**; high certainty versus dopamine, low certainty versus epinephrine
  and selepressin.

### Antimicrobials

- **Septic shock: administer antimicrobial therapy immediately, ideally within 1 hour** —
  **strong, very low certainty**.
- **Sepsis without shock: within 1 hour** — **strong, very low certainty**.
- **Possible sepsis without shock: within 3 hours** of suspicion — **conditional, very low
  certainty**.

### Source control

- **Early over late source control, ideally within 6 hours** — **conditional, very low
  certainty**.

### Corticosteroids

- **IV corticosteroids in septic shock** — **conditional, low certainty**.

### After resuscitation

- **Active fluid removal** (diuretics or ultrafiltration) after the acute resuscitation phase
  — **conditional, very low certainty**. *(New in 2026.)*

## What is new versus 2021 (per ESICM)

The 2026 edition expands beyond early recognition and treatment into "antibiotic
optimisation, blood pressure targets in older adults, fluid removal after resuscitation, and
continuity of care post-discharge — including psychological and physical rehabilitation."

The paediatric guidelines (updated from 2020, not 2021) add a **conditional recommendation
for point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS)** to guide resuscitation, and address post-sepsis
morbidity: "30–40% of children surviving ICU-level sepsis treatment may face lasting health
issues."
