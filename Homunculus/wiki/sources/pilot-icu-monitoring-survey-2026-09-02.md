# Pilot ICU monitoring survey (2026-09-02)

**What it is.** A first-party site survey: the pilot hospital's ICU continuous-monitoring
landscape (Dräger Infinity central + 13 Kappa VF9.1-C bedsides + 12 idle M300 telemetry units,
subnet 10.10.203.0/24), transcribed from device screens. Photographs deliberately not stored
(patient waveforms, ward backgrounds, service credentials); the text capture is the record.

**Authority.** Low — first-party observation on a single day, not a published source. It
supports **no clinical claims**; it is engineering evidence for integration planning only.

**Key facts it supports.**
- The pilot site already produces continuous ICU telemetry on 13 beds; the HL7 exit for this
  Dräger generation is Infinity Gateway (presence/licensing unresolved).
- The site runs at UTC+4 (Asia/Baku) — the 2026-08-28 "clock discrepancy" was a wrong timezone
  assumption on our side, not a site fault.

**Limitations.** Inventory and versions can drift; the Gateway question is open; nothing here
may be cited for clinical reasoning.
