# Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2026

**What it is.** The current international guideline for managing sepsis and septic shock in
adults (Prescott, Antonelli, Alhazzani, et al., *Crit Care Med* 2026;54(4):725–812). 129
statements, 46 of them new; GRADE methodology; evidence through June 2025. Supersedes
[[surviving-sepsis-campaign-2021]].

**Authority: high** — but see the capture caveat below, which limits how far this note can be
leaned on.

**Capture caveat.** The full text is paywalled and the PubMed record has no abstract. What is
in `raw/` was assembled from SCCM's own guideline page and ESICM's announcement. The
recommendations captured are accurately stated; **the other ~110 statements are simply not
here.** Never infer from this capture that the guideline is silent on something.

## Key claims it supports

- **qSOFA is out as a screening tool.** Use NEWS, NEWS2, MEWS or SIRS *over* qSOFA — strong,
  moderate certainty. → [[sepsis-screening-tools]], [[qsofa]]
- **≥30 mL/kg crystalloid within 3 hours** for sepsis-induced hypoperfusion or septic shock —
  conditional, low certainty; reassess frequently. → [[initial-resuscitation-sepsis]]
- **MAP target 65 mmHg** — strong, moderate certainty. **In patients ≥65 years, 60–65 mmHg** —
  conditional, low certainty (new in 2026). → [[map-target-sepsis]]
- **Norepinephrine first-line** over dopamine, epinephrine, selepressin — strong. →
  [[vasopressors-in-septic-shock]]
- **Antimicrobials within 1 hour for septic shock AND for sepsis without shock** — both strong,
  very low certainty. Within 3 hours for *possible* sepsis without shock — conditional. This is
  a **change from 2021**, where sepsis without shock was a weak 3-hour recommendation. →
  [[antimicrobial-timing-sepsis]]
- **Serial lactate to guide resuscitation** — conditional, low certainty; do not chase complete
  normalisation. → [[lactate-in-sepsis]]
- **Source control ideally within 6 hours** — conditional, very low certainty. →
  [[source-control-sepsis]]
- **IV corticosteroids in septic shock** — conditional, low certainty.
- **Active fluid removal after the resuscitation phase** — conditional, very low certainty (new
  in 2026). → [[fluid-overload-icu]]

## Limitations that matter to Homunculus

- **Most recommendations rest on low or very low certainty evidence.** The 1-hour antimicrobial
  recommendation is *strong* but rated **very low certainty** — a strong recommendation on weak
  evidence, driven by the cost of being wrong. Homunculus must transmit that nuance, not flatten
  it into a confident-sounding alert. See [[grade-methodology]].
- **Nothing in this capture addresses automated screening, ML, or clinical decision support.**
  Whether the full guideline does is **unknown**. This is the most important open question for
  the project and the reason to obtain the full text — see [[open-questions]].
