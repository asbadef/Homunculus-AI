# G2-B5b «Ertele» Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A person who may acknowledge an alarm can snooze an unanswered high (≤30 min) or moderate (≤120 min) alarm on the
live system, with a named reason. While snoozed the alarm makes no sound, leaves the waiting queue and does not escalate;
at its `until` it sounds again, on a fresh escalation clock with the doctor's page cleared.

**Architecture:**
- **Engine** (live in-memory system, as G2-B5 built on).
  - `api/snooze.py` holds the owner's rules as pure functions.
  - `AckStore.snoozes` holds the state. `escalate()` skips snoozed keys. The escalation cycle ends expired snoozes
    (fresh clock from `until`, page cleared) and republishes the card.
  - `POST /alerts/snooze` writes an `alert_snooze` audit row, then the store. The card's alerts carry `snooze`.
  - An acknowledgement, the episode's end, a discharge or the same alarm worse ends a snooze. `rehydrate` replays it.
- **Dashboard.**
  - `data/snooze.js` mirrors the rules, and `needsAnswer` leaves a snoozed alarm out until `until` by the board's clock.
  - A small hook (`useSnoozeClock`) re-reads that clock the moment a snooze ends, for the bell, the rail badge, the sound,
    the lock screen and the beds screen's waiting line.
  - The alarm screen gets «Ertele», its dialog, an «Ertelenenler» group and the critical note. The audit page gets a label.
- **No migration.** In memory plus the audit trail, like B5. The Postgres `escalation/` package stays untouched.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 / FastAPI / pytest (engine); React 18 / Vite / Vitest / Testing Library (dashboard); nginx map.

## Plan notes (readings the spec left open, resolved toward the B5 precedent)

1. **A worse alarm ends its snooze.** `news2` is one key whose severity moves (NEWS2 5–6 moderate/`soon`, ≥7
   high/`immediate`).
   - Kept, a 120-minute moderate snooze would silence a NEWS2 ≥7 high alarm past the owner's 30-minute cap, and keep an
     immediate alarm from escalating.
   - So the store records the severity a snooze was given at. `sync_alerts` ends the snooze when the same alarm's
     severity rises above it.
   - The alarm then waits as if never snoozed: its clock and page are untouched. A fresh clock would escalate a
     worsened alarm later than an unsnoozed one.
   - This only ever ends a snooze earlier. **Flag for the owner.**
2. **A snooze that ends with its episode** (`prune`) leaves the X13 chain exactly as any unanswered episode does: the
   chain's clock and page as they stood. The spec says only that the snooze does not ride the chain. The return sounds,
   and escalates on the chain's clock (X13 unchanged).
3. **Replay expiry is judged once, at the end of the replay**, against the restart's `now` (`rehydrate(…, now=)`).
   - A replayed snooze whose `until` has passed is ended with the clock rule at expiry (`clock_from = until`). `paged`
     is not replayed (the existing limitation).
   - Limitation (written in the docstring): if the log shows a snooze expiring and then the alarm worsening, the restart
     keeps the pre-snooze clock. Escalation comes sooner, never later.
4. **Refusal order and words.**
   - The reason is judged before the scope check, as `/alerts/rrt-call` judges its reason: it names nothing of the
     patient.
   - `minutes` is judged after the alarm's severity is known.
   - low/info share `snooze_not_allowed` with critical and latched.
   - The exact details are in «The shared contract» below.
5. **The dashboard's clock.**
   - The spec says «the existing board clock ticks the re-render». The bell, the rail badge and the sound have no ticking
     clock today, and a per-second tick would re-render the rail and the signals every second.
   - So `useSnoozeClock` reads the same skew-corrected board clock as `useBoardNow`, but re-reads it only when the next
     snooze ends (one timer).
   - The alarm and RRT screens already tick each second; they use their own `useBoardNow`.
   - With no clock (`nowMs` absent), no snooze is honoured: the alarm waits.
   - A snooze on any severity but high/moderate is never honoured client-side. This is the floor `NEVER_SUPPRESSED` keeps
     for chronic-expected.
6. **«Kritik alarm ertelenemez» once.** It sits in the action column of the first critical alarm that waits, for
   holders of `alerts.ack`.
7. **No Turkish suffix on a time.** The spec's «hh:mm'e kadar» cannot take a fixed suffix (vowel harmony follows the
   number). The row says «Ertelendi · bitiş {until} · {by} · {reason}», the guide's «imza: {time}» pattern. For OTHER,
   the reason shown is the person's own explanation.
8. **«Ertelenenler» renders only when it holds an alarm.** The patient-screen «Ertelendi» note (D5, «may») is not built.
9. **Task order differs from a strict «engine → tables last».**
   - The route tables (Task 5) come right after the route, so no commit leaves the drift guards red.
   - On the dashboard side the strings come first (Task 9): every screen test reads Azerbaijani text.
   - The snooze refusals map to `ertele.err.*` keys in the slice's own namespace. `API_MESSAGE_KEYS` takes whole keys,
     as G3-02's `gorevler.err.*` do.

## The shared contract (both halves build against this; do not drift from it)

- **Route:** `POST /alerts/snooze`.
  - Body: `{patient_id, kind, minutes, reason, reason_text}`. `reason_text` may be absent or `null`.
  - Answer: 200 with the patient's board card, as `/ack` and `/alerts/rrt-call` answer.
- **Card:** every alert, firing or latched, carries `snooze`: `{by, at, until, minutes, reason, reason_text}` or `null`.
  - `by` is the snoozing person's username.
  - `at` and `until` are ISO-8601 UTC strings with an offset (`2026-10-25T09:01:00+00:00`).
  - `minutes` is an int. `reason` is one of the five codes. `reason_text` is the trimmed text, or `null`.
  - A latched alert (`resolved_at` set) always has `snooze: null`.
  - An expired snooze may stay on a card for up to one escalation interval (20 s), until the cycle republishes. The
    dashboard judges `until` on its own clock.
- **Reasons, in the dialog's order:** `CLINICAL_DECISION_MADE`, `ARTEFACT_OR_SIGNAL_NOISE`, `PROCEDURE_IN_PROGRESS`,
  `PATIENT_BASELINE`, `OTHER`.
- **Caps:** high 1–30, moderate 1–120. The dialog offers high 5/10/15/30 and moderate 15/30/60/120.
- **Refusals**, in the order the route checks them, with the exact `detail` strings:

  | status | `detail` | when |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 401 | `missing bearer token` / `unknown credential` | no credential |
  | 403 | `alarmı bir kişi erteler` | a device token |
  | 403 | `permission required: alerts.ack` | no `alerts.ack` |
  | 400 | `geçersiz patient_id` / `geçersiz kind` | malformed body |
  | 422 | `erteleme gerekçesi listeden olmalı; «Diğer» için açıklama 15–500 karakter` | reason not on the list; OTHER under 15 characters; a text over 500 or not a string |
  | 503 / 404 | `kapsam şu anda okunamıyor` / `hasta bulunamadı` | the caller's scope (existing `_require_patient`) |
  | 404 | `no such active alert` | neither firing nor latched |
  | 409 | `snooze_not_allowed` | latched; critical; low; info |
  | 409 | `alarm zaten yanıtlandı; ertelenmez` | acknowledged |
  | 422 | `erteleme süresi bu alarm için izin verilen aralıkta değil` | `minutes` not a whole number in 1..cap |

- **Audit row:** `event_type="alert_snooze"`, with `patient_id` and `subject=kind`.
  - Payload: `{role, via: "user", actor, groups, minutes, until, reason, at}`.
  - `reason_text` is added only when one was given.
- **Dashboard alarm** (`liveAdapters.alertsFromPatients`):
  `snooze: {by, atMs, untilMs, minutes, reason, reasonText} | null`.

```python
# src/homunculus/api/snooze.py
"""G2-B5b «Ertele» (spec 2026-11-10-g2b-b5b-ertele-design.md): the rules a snooze of a live alarm is held to.

Owner D1 (2026-11-10): the rules already in the code — the Postgres `escalation/service.py` `snooze_alert` (§6.2,
S-22), which stays dead and untouched — applied to the live system: critical never; high at most 30 minutes;
moderate at most 120; low and info none (they make no sound, so there is nothing to silence); a reason from a fixed
list, OTHER with at least 15 characters of free text.

Pure: no store, no clock, no HTTP. The route (`POST /alerts/snooze`) and the replay (`rehydrate`) ask the same
functions, so a restart judges a row as the live door judged it."""
from __future__ import annotations

#: The most minutes each severity may be snoozed for (owner D1). A severity not here is never snoozed.
SNOOZE_CAPS: dict[str, int] = {"high": 30, "moderate": 120}

#: The reasons a snooze may give: the Postgres `SnoozeReason` codes (§6.2).
OTHER = "OTHER"
SNOOZE_REASONS = frozenset({"CLINICAL_DECISION_MADE", "ARTEFACT_OR_SIGNAL_NOISE", "PROCEDURE_IN_PROGRESS",
                            "PATIENT_BASELINE", OTHER})
#: OTHER's free text, trimmed, is at least this long (§6.2) ...
OTHER_MIN_CHARS = 15
#: ... and no text is longer than this: an RRT call's ceiling (G2-B5), short enough for a card.
REASON_TEXT_MAX = 500

#: The engine's severities, mildest first (reco/engine.py). Plan note 1: the same alarm worse ends its snooze.
_RANK = ("info", "low", "moderate", "high", "critical")


def snooze_cap(severity: object) -> int | None:
    """The most minutes an alarm of `severity` may be snoozed for, or None: never (critical, low, info, unknown)."""
    return SNOOZE_CAPS.get(severity) if isinstance(severity, str) else None


def minutes_ok(severity: object, minutes: object) -> bool:
    """`minutes` is a whole number from 1 to the severity's cap: never a bool, a float or a text."""
    cap = snooze_cap(severity)
    return (cap is not None and isinstance(minutes, int) and not isinstance(minutes, bool)
            and 1 <= minutes <= cap)


def clean_reason(reason: object, text: object) -> tuple[str, str | None] | None:
    """`(reason, text)` as the door keeps them (the text trimmed; None when blank or absent), or None when the pair is
    refused: a reason not on the list, a text that is not a string or is longer than REASON_TEXT_MAX once trimmed,
    or OTHER without OTHER_MIN_CHARS of text."""
    if not isinstance(reason, str) or reason not in SNOOZE_REASONS:
        return None
    if text is None:
        cleaned = None
    elif isinstance(text, str):
        cleaned = text.strip() or None
    else:
        return None
    if cleaned is not None and len(cleaned) > REASON_TEXT_MAX:
        return None
    if reason == OTHER and (cleaned is None or len(cleaned) < OTHER_MIN_CHARS):
        return None
    return reason, cleaned


def worse(severity: object, than: object) -> bool:
    """Is `severity` worse than `than`? A severity this build does not know counts as worse: it may be the worst."""
    if severity not in _RANK:
        return True
    return than in _RANK and _RANK.index(severity) > _RANK.index(than)
```

```python
from collections.abc import Container
from datetime import datetime, timedelta

from homunculus.reco.engine import Alert


def escalate(
    alerts: list[tuple[str, Alert]],
    acks: dict[tuple[str, str], dict],
    first_seen: dict[tuple[str, str], datetime],
    now: datetime,
    threshold: timedelta,
    snoozed: Container[tuple[str, str]] = frozenset(),
) -> set[tuple[str, str]]:
    """`snoozed` (G2-B5b) are the keys a person snoozed: they wait, unescalated, until their snooze ends."""
    escalated: set[tuple[str, str]] = set()
    for patient_id, alert in alerts:
        if alert.urgency != "immediate":
            continue
        key = (patient_id, alert.kind)
        if key in acks or key in snoozed:
            continue
        seen = first_seen.get(key)
        if seen is not None and now - seen >= threshold:
            escalated.add(key)
    return escalated
```

```python
    @app.post("/alerts/snooze")
    async def snooze_alert(authorization: str | None = Header(default=None), body: dict = Body(...)):
        """«Ertele»: a person snoozes one active alarm for a bounded time, with a named reason (G2-B5b, spec
        2026-11-10; owner D1: critical never, high at most 30 minutes, moderate at most 120, low and info none).

        Keyed like `/ack`: patient and kind in the body, never in the path the access log writes. Whoever may
        acknowledge may snooze (`alerts.ack`, nurses included). A device is refused explicitly, since a device is
        exempt from permission checks: a snooze is a person's decision, named in the trail.

        Refused:
        - an alarm not on the board (404);
        - a latched critical, a critical, a low or an info alarm (409 `snooze_not_allowed`);
        - an answered alarm (409);
        - minutes outside 1..cap, a reason the list does not hold, OTHER under 15 characters, or a text over 500 (422).
        The reason is judged before the patient's scope, as the RRT call judges its reason: it names nothing of the
        patient. A second snooze replaces the first, with its own `until`. The trail before memory, as `/ack`
        (spec 2026-11-01, D3).
        """
        caller = await _caller(authorization)
        if caller.is_device:
            raise HTTPException(status_code=403, detail="alarmı bir kişi erteler")
        _require(caller, ALERTS_ACK)
        hospital = caller.hospital
        engine = _engine(hospital)
        store = _store(hospital)
        patient_id, kind = _body_patient_id(body), body.get("kind")
        if not isinstance(kind, str):
            raise HTTPException(status_code=400, detail="geçersiz kind")
        cleaned = clean_reason(body.get("reason"), body.get("reason_text"))
        if cleaned is None:
            raise HTTPException(status_code=422,
                                detail="erteleme gerekçesi listeden olmalı; «Diğer» için açıklama 15–500 karakter")
        reason, reason_text = cleaned
        who = _attribution(caller, body)
        await _require_patient(caller, authorization, patient_id)
        key = (patient_id, kind)
        if key not in live_keys(engine, store):
            raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail="no such active alert")
        if key in store.resolved:
            # A latched critical (G2-A1): critical, and its rule has stopped. There is nothing left to silence.
            raise HTTPException(status_code=409, detail="snooze_not_allowed")
        if key in store.acks:
            raise HTTPException(status_code=409, detail="alarm zaten yanıtlandı; ertelenmez")
        severity = next(a.severity for a in engine.active_alerts(patient_id) if a.kind == kind)
        if snooze_cap(severity) is None:
            raise HTTPException(status_code=409, detail="snooze_not_allowed")
        minutes = body.get("minutes")
        if not minutes_ok(severity, minutes):
            raise HTTPException(status_code=422, detail="erteleme süresi bu alarm için izin verilen aralıkta değil")
        now = datetime.now(timezone.utc)
        until = now + timedelta(minutes=minutes)
        audit.append(AuditEvent(hospital, "alert_snooze", patient_id, kind,
                                {**who, "minutes": minutes, "until": until.isoformat(), "reason": reason,
                                 **({"reason_text": reason_text} if reason_text is not None else {}),
                                 "at": now.isoformat()}))
        store.snooze(patient_id, kind, by=who["actor"], at=now, minutes=minutes, reason=reason,
                     reason_text=reason_text, severity=severity)
        board = _card(hospital, patient_id)
        await broadcaster.publish(hospital, board)
        return board
```

```js
// src/live/data/snooze.js — «Ertele» on the dashboard (G2-B5b, spec 2026-11-10-g2b-b5b-ertele-design.md): the owner's
// rules as the screens need them, and whether an alarm is snoozed NOW.
//
// THE ENGINE DECIDES (POST /alerts/snooze). These mirror its rules only so the alarm screen offers what the engine
// takes: critical never; high at most 30 minutes; moderate at most 120; low and info none (owner D1). The dialog's
// durations are the spec's (D4): high 5/10/15/30, moderate 15/30/60/120.
//
// A SNOOZED ALARM is one whose `snooze.untilMs` is still ahead of the board's clock, and only a high or moderate one.
// A snooze on any other severity, a critical above all, is never honoured, whatever a card carries: the floor
// boardStore's `NEVER_SUPPRESSED` keeps for the chronic-expected flag. Without a clock (`nowMs` not a number), nothing
// is snoozed: an alarm the screen cannot place in time waits, and sounds.

/** The severities a snooze is honoured on. A Set, never an object lookup: a card's severity string must not reach
 *  `constructor` on a prototype. */
const SNOOZABLE = new Set(["high", "moderate"]);

/** The durations the dialog offers, by severity, each within the owner's cap. */
export const SNOOZE_CHOICES = Object.freeze({
  high: Object.freeze([5, 10, 15, 30]),
  moderate: Object.freeze([15, 30, 60, 120]),
});

/** The engine's reason codes, in the order the dialog lists them. */
export const SNOOZE_REASONS = Object.freeze([
  "CLINICAL_DECISION_MADE", "ARTEFACT_OR_SIGNAL_NOISE", "PROCEDURE_IN_PROGRESS", "PATIENT_BASELINE", "OTHER",
]);
export const OTHER = "OTHER";
/** «Diğer»'s explanation, trimmed: at least this long (the engine's rule) ... */
export const OTHER_MIN_CHARS = 15;
/** ... and no explanation longer than this. */
export const REASON_TEXT_MAX = 500;

/** May this alarm be offered «Ertele»? An unacknowledged, still-firing high or moderate one. A snoozed one may be
 *  snoozed again: the new snooze replaces the old one. */
export function maySnooze(alarm) {
  return !!alarm && !alarm.ack && alarm.status === "FIRED" && SNOOZABLE.has(alarm.severity);
}

/** Is this alarm snoozed at `nowMs`, the board's clock? */
export function isSnoozed(alarm, nowMs) {
  const until = alarm?.snooze?.untilMs;
  return Number.isFinite(nowMs) && Number.isFinite(until) && until > nowMs && SNOOZABLE.has(alarm?.severity);
}

/** The next instant after `nowMs` at which a snooze on the board ends, or null. */
export function nextSnoozeEnd(alerts, nowMs) {
  let next = null;
  for (const alarm of Array.isArray(alerts) ? alerts : []) {
    if (!isSnoozed(alarm, nowMs)) continue;
    if (next === null || alarm.snooze.untilMs < next) next = alarm.snooze.untilMs;
  }
  return next;
}
```

```js
// src/live/data/useSnoozeClock.js — the board's clock for the waiting queue (G2-B5b, spec D4, plan note 5).
//
// A snoozed alarm is out of the waiting queue (boardStore `needsAnswer`) until its `snooze.untilMs`, judged on THIS
// clock. It is the browser's clock corrected by the server's skew, `useBoardNow`'s rule. It is re-read only the moment
// the next snooze on the board ends: one timer, not a tick. So the bell, the rail's count, the sound, the lock screen
// and the beds screen take the alarm back on time without a per-second re-render, and without waiting for the
// server's republish (the escalation cycle sends one too, within its interval). Between those moments the value
// holds still, so it is safe in an effect's dependencies.
import { useEffect, useState } from "react";
import { useBoard } from "./BoardProvider.jsx";
import { nextSnoozeEnd } from "./snooze.js";

/** A margin past the snooze's end, so the re-read lands after it rather than a millisecond before. */
const PAST_THE_END_MS = 50;
/** The longest delay a timer keeps; a longer one would fire at once (the browser's 32-bit limit). */
const MAX_TIMER_MS = 2_147_483_647;

/** The board's instant (ms) as the waiting queue should judge snoozes by. */
export function useSnoozeClock() {
  const { alerts, clockSkewMs } = useBoard();
  const skew = Number.isFinite(clockSkewMs) ? clockSkewMs : 0;
  const [readAt, setReadAt] = useState(() => Date.now());
  const nowMs = readAt + skew;
  const next = nextSnoozeEnd(alerts, nowMs);
  useEffect(() => {
    if (next === null) return undefined;
    const wait = Math.min(MAX_TIMER_MS, Math.max(0, next - (Date.now() + skew)) + PAST_THE_END_MS);
    const timer = setTimeout(() => setReadAt(Date.now()), wait);
    return () => clearTimeout(timer);
  }, [next, skew, readAt]);
  return nowMs;
}
```

```jsx
/** «Sounds again at»: the minute it names must be right while the dialog stays open. */
const TICK_MS = 1_000;

/** Each reason's words, by the engine's code. The keys are literal, so rawKeys.test.jsx checks every one exists. */
export const REASON_KEYS = Object.freeze({
  CLINICAL_DECISION_MADE: "ertele.reason.clinical",
  ARTEFACT_OR_SIGNAL_NOISE: "ertele.reason.artefact",
  PROCEDURE_IN_PROGRESS: "ertele.reason.procedure",
  PATIENT_BASELINE: "ertele.reason.baseline",
  OTHER: "ertele.reason.other",
});

/** A snooze's reason as a row says it: «Diğer»'s own explanation (the person's words), the list's words otherwise,
 *  and a code this build does not know as it came. */
export function snoozeReasonText(t, snooze) {
  if (!snooze) return "";
  if (snooze.reason === OTHER && snooze.reasonText) return snooze.reasonText;
  return Object.prototype.hasOwnProperty.call(REASON_KEYS, snooze.reason)
    ? t(REASON_KEYS[snooze.reason])
    : String(snooze.reason ?? "—");
}

/** The snooze's end that the engine's answer (a board entry, raw) carries for `kind`, in ms, or null. */
function untilOn(entry, kind) {
  const alert = Array.isArray(entry?.alerts) ? entry.alerts.find((a) => a?.kind === kind) : null;
  const ms = Date.parse(alert?.snooze?.until ?? "");
  return Number.isNaN(ms) ? null : ms;
}

/** `alarm` the board alarm to snooze; `open` false renders nothing. A new alarm, or a new opening, starts afresh. */
export function SnoozeDialog({ alarm, open, onClose }) {
  if (!open || !alarm) return null;
  return <SnoozeForm key={alarm.id} alarm={alarm} onClose={onClose} />;
}

function SnoozeForm({ alarm, onClose }) {
  const { t } = useI18n();
  const board = useBoard();
  const toast = useToast();
  const nowMs = useBoardNow(TICK_MS);
  const choices = SNOOZE_CHOICES[alarm.severity] ?? [];
  const [minutes, setMinutes] = useState(choices[0] ?? null);
  const [reason, setReason] = useState("");
  const [text, setText] = useState("");
  const [tried, setTried] = useState(false);   // a problem shows once she has pressed «Ertele»
  const [busy, setBusy] = useState(false);
  const sending = useRef(false);
  const reasonBox = useRef(null);
  const textBox = useRef(null);
  const formId = useId();
  const whatId = useId();
  const bed = alarm.bedLabel || alarm.patientId;
  const subject = `${bed} · ${alarm.kind}`;
  const other = reason === OTHER;
  const noReason = !SNOOZE_REASONS.includes(reason);
  const shortText = other && text.trim().length < OTHER_MIN_CHARS;

  async function send() {
    if (sending.current || busy || minutes === null) return;
    if (noReason || shortText) {
      // Said under its control, and the focus taken there; the server is not asked. flushSync: the problem must be in
      // the DOM, tied to the control, when the focus arrives (VitalsForm).
      flushSync(() => setTried(true));
      (noReason ? reasonBox : textBox).current?.focus();
      return;
    }
    setTried(true);
    sending.current = true;
    setBusy(true);
    let said = null;                             // [text, tone] once the server has answered
    try {
      const entry = await board.store.snooze(alarm.patientId, alarm.kind, minutes, reason, other ? text.trim() : null);
      const until = untilOn(entry, alarm.kind);
      said = [t("ertele.done", { subject, time: until === null ? "—" : hhmm(until) })];
    } catch (err) {
      // A request the session's end cut off: nobody is there to read an answer.
      if (err?.superseded) said = null;
      else if (mayHaveLanded(err)) said = [t("ertele.mayHaveLanded", { subject }), "warn"];
      else said = [t("ertele.failed", { subject, reason: apiErrorText(t, err) }), "error"];
    } finally {
      sending.current = false;
      setBusy(false);
    }
    if (said) toast(...said);
    onClose();
  }

  return (
    <Modal
      open title={t("ertele.title", { bed })} onClose={onClose} busy={busy}
      actions={(
        <>
          <Button variant="ghost" onClick={onClose} busy={busy}>{t("ertele.cancel")}</Button>
          <Button variant="primary" icon="clock" onClick={send} busy={busy}>{t("ertele.send")}</Button>
        </>
      )}
    >
      {/* Enter in the explanation box presses «Ertele»: the same checks. */}
      <form id={formId} noValidate onSubmit={(event) => { event.preventDefault(); send(); }}>
        {/* Which alarm, when a bed has more than one: the engine's own English summary, and its kind. */}
        <p id={whatId} className="m-0 mb-3 text-13 text-ink">
          <span lang="en">{summaryOf(alarm) ?? t("board.findingUnnamed")}</span>
          <span className="font-data text-mute">{` · ${alarm.kind}`}</span>
        </p>
        <div className="mb-2.5 grid gap-1.5">
          <Seg label={t("ertele.duration")} value={minutes} onChange={setMinutes}
            options={choices.map((m) => ({ value: m, label: t("ertele.minutes", { count: m }) }))} />
          {minutes !== null ? (
            <p className="m-0 text-12.5 text-mute">{t("ertele.soundsAgain", { time: hhmm(nowMs + minutes * 60_000) })}</p>
          ) : null}
        </div>
        <Field label={t("ertele.reasonLabel")} layout="col" required
          error={tried && noReason ? t("ertele.reasonMissing") : undefined}>
          <Select ref={reasonBox} value={reason} onChange={(event) => setReason(event.target.value)} disabled={busy}
            aria-describedby={whatId}>
            <option value="">{t("ertele.reasonPick")}</option>
            {SNOOZE_REASONS.map((code) => <option key={code} value={code}>{t(REASON_KEYS[code])}</option>)}
          </Select>
        </Field>
        {other ? (
          <Field label={t("ertele.text")} layout="col" required hint={t("ertele.textHint")}
            error={tried && shortText ? t("ertele.textShort") : undefined}>
            <Input ref={textBox} autoComplete="off" maxLength={REASON_TEXT_MAX} value={text}
              onChange={(event) => setText(event.target.value)} readOnly={busy} />
          </Field>
        ) : null}
        <p className="m-0 text-12 text-mute">{t("ertele.trail")}</p>
      </form>
      {/* A busy button reads only "unavailable"; the words come from here, as on the alarm screen. */}
      <Announce text={busy ? t("ertele.sending") : ""} />
    </Modal>
  );
}
```
