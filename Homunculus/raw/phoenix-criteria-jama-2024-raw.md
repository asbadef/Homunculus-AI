# Phoenix Criteria — Pediatric Sepsis and Septic Shock (JAMA 2024)

Replaces the 2005 SIRS-based paediatric sepsis definition. Sepsis is defined by **organ
dysfunction**, in the same spirit as Sepsis-3 for adults, but with a paediatric-specific score.

## Definitions

- **Sepsis:** suspected infection **plus Phoenix Sepsis Score ≥2**.
- **Septic shock:** sepsis **plus ≥1 cardiovascular point**.

## Phoenix Sepsis Score (4 organ systems, max 13 points)

### Respiratory (0–3)

| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 0 | PaO₂/FiO₂ ≥400 or SpO₂/FiO₂ ≥292 |
| 1 | PaO₂/FiO₂ <400 on respiratory support, or SpO₂/FiO₂ <292 on any support |
| 2 | PaO₂/FiO₂ 100–200 with invasive mechanical ventilation, or SpO₂/FiO₂ 148–220 with IMV |
| 3 | PaO₂/FiO₂ <100 with IMV, or SpO₂/FiO₂ <148 with IMV |

### Cardiovascular (0–6) — three components scored independently, up to 2 points each

| Component | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|---|---|
| Vasoactive medications | 1 agent | ≥2 agents |
| Lactate | 5–10.9 mmol/L | ≥11 mmol/L |
| MAP (age-adjusted) | severe hypotension by age band (infant → 17 y) | more severe by age band |

### Coagulation (0–2) — 1 point each, capped at 2

- Platelets <100 ×10³/µL
- INR >1.3
- D-dimer >2 mg/L FEU
- Fibrinogen <100 mg/dL

### Neurological (0–2)

| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 0 | GCS >10 with reactive pupils |
| 1 | GCS ≤10 |
| 2 | Bilaterally fixed pupils |

## Population — CRITICAL SCOPE LIMIT

**Applies to:** children **<18 years**.

**Explicitly EXCLUDED:**
- **Preterm neonates (<37 weeks' gestation at birth)**
- **Newborns who remained hospitalised after birth**
- Birth hospitalisations
- Children whose postconceptional age is <37 weeks

**These criteria therefore do not apply to the NICU population.**

## Mortality associated with the criteria

| Group | Higher-resource setting | Lower-resource setting |
|---|---|---|
| Sepsis (Phoenix ≥2) | 7.1% (726/10,243) | 28.5% (441/1,549) |
| Septic shock | 10.8% (593/5,502) | 33.5% (422/1,260) |
| Sepsis with remote organ dysfunction | 8.0% (700/8,728) | 32.3% (427/1,320) |

## Stated limitations

- Simplifies "complex biological processes."
- Infection identification relies on **proxy markers** (microbiology results, antibiotic
  administration) rather than a gold standard.
- Not validated on multiomics data.
- Higher-resource derivation data came **only from US children's hospitals**.
- Mortality endpoint omits infection-associated **morbidity**.
- The 24-hour window used excluded healthcare-associated infections.
- **The score incorporates treatments given in response to sepsis** (vasoactive agents,
  ventilation) — it partly measures the response, not only the disease.
- **Preterm neonates and term newborns excluded entirely.**
